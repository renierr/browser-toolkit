Treadmill Control

Monitor and control your treadmill via Bluetooth FTMS.

Connection problems?
  • Ensure the treadmill is NOT connected to another app/phone.
  • Turn Bluetooth off and on in your system settings.
  • Restart the treadmill (power cycle).
  • If it says "NetworkError", try unpairing/forgetting the device from your system's Bluetooth settings first.
