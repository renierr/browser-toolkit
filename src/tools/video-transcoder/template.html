Video Transcoder

Convert video files to different formats or compress them. All processing happens locally in your browser.
Since this is local in your Browser keep in mind that this transcoder is not meant for very large files. It is best suited for small or medium sized files.
The transcoder uses FFmpeg to transcode the video. with the WASM version of FFmpeg. So no GPU acceleration is used, all runs in CPU.
