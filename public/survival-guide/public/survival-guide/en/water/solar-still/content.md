# Building a Solar Still

A solar still captures moisture vaporizing from the ground or vegetation, condensing it on a plastic sheet, and collecting it in a container. It operates perfectly even in arid deserts, utilizing the sun's energy.

## What You Need
- A clean, clear plastic sheet (at least 5x5 feet / 1.5x1.5m)
- A digging tool
- A container or cup to catch the water
- A small rock
- Green vegetation (optional, but highly recommended)

## Steps to Build

1. **Select a Location**
   Dig in an area that gets full sunlight all day. Areas where the ground is damp, or inside dried river beds, yield more water.

2. **Dig the Hole**
   Dig a hole about 3 feet (90cm) across and 2 feet (60cm) deep. The sides should slope downwards toward the center.

3. **Place the Container**
   Place your cup or container in the dead center at the bottom of the hole.

4. **Add Vegetation (Optional)**
   Line the sides of the hole with chopped green leaves, cactus, or even urine (pour it into the soil *around* the cup, NOT in it). The sun will extract pure water from these sources.

5. **Cover the Hole**
   Lay the plastic sheet over the hole. Use soil and rocks around the edges to seal it completely tight against the ground. No moisture should escape.

6. **Place the Weight**
   Place a small rock directly in the center of the plastic sheet, so it points directly above the collection cup. The plastic should form an inverted cone.

7. **Wait**
   The sun will heat the hole, turning moisture into vapor. The vapor condenses on the underside of the plastic, runs down the slope toward the center weight, and drips into the cup.

:::tip
Set up multiple smaller stills rather than one massive one to increase your yield. 
:::

:::warning
A solar still requires significant energy to dig. If your yield is less than the sweat you lost digging it, you are losing hydration. Dig in the cool of the morning or evening.
:::

## Checklist
- [ ] Dig a cone-shaped hole in direct sunlight
- [ ] Place collection cup directly in the center
- [ ] Line hole with vegetation to increase yield
- [ ] Seal the edges of the plastic completely with dirt
- [ ] Place weight perfectly over the cup
