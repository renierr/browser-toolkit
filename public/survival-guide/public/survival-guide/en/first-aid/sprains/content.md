# Treating Sprains (R.I.C.E. Method)

A sprain happens when ligaments (tissues connecting bone to bone) are stretched or torn, most commonly in the ankle. A strain is an injury to a muscle or tendon. Both are debilitating in the wild.

## Diagnosis: Is it broken?

If the injured person cannot bear ANY weight on the injured limb, hears a loud "crack" during the injury, or the limb is clearly deformed, treat it as a fracture (see the [Treating Fractures](#first-aid/fracture) guide) and splint it immediately.

If they can bear some weight but it hurts and swells, it's likely a sprain.

## The R.I.C.E. Treatment

For the first 48 hours after a soft tissue injury, follow this protocol:

### 1. Rest (R)
Stop moving immediately. Do not attempt to "walk it off." Taking the weight off the injury prevents further damage. If you must move, use a walking stick or have a companion provide support.

### 2. Ice (I)
Cool the area to reduce swelling and pain. 
- In the wild, use a wet cloth, snow, or submerge the limb in a cold stream for 15-20 minutes at a time.
- Repeat every 2-3 hours.
- *Never apply ice (if you have it) directly to the skin.*

### 3. Compression (C)
Wrap the injured joint firmly with an elastic bandage (ACE wrap), shirt, or bandana.
- Start wrapping from the point furthest away from the heart and move inwards (e.g., toes towards the calf).
- Wrap firmly, but not so tight that it cuts off circulation. 
- Loosen the wrap if toes/fingers turn blue, feel cold, or tingle.

### 4. Elevation (E)
Prop the injured limb up so it is higher than the victim's heart to drain fluid away via gravity and reduce swelling.

:::warning
Do not apply heat to a new sprain. Heat increases blood flow, which will dramatically increase swelling for the first 48 hours.
:::

## Returning to Movement
If you are in a survival situation and *must* hike out:
1. Heavily wrap the joint with a supportive figure-8 bandage, or tightly lace the stiffest boot available over the ankle.
2. Construct a walking stick or crutch.
3. Take frequent breaks to elevate the limb.

## Checklist
- [ ] Verify the bone isn't clearly broken or deformed
- [ ] Remove weight from the injured limb immediately
- [ ] Cool the joint with water/snow for 15 minutes
- [ ] Wrap with a figure-8 compression bandage
- [ ] Elevate above the level of the heart
