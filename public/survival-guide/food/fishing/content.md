# Improvised Fishing

Fishing can provide food without needing to hunt. Here are ways to catch fish with minimal equipment.

## Simple Fishing Line

1. Find strong thread, cord, or plant fiber
2. Attach hook (bent needle, twig hook, bone)
3. Add weight (stone, piece of metal)
4. Use bait (insect, worm, small piece of meat)

## Fish Traps

### Basket Trap

1. Weave sticks or branches into cone shape
2. Place bait inside
3. Funnel opening facing upstream
4. Set in slow-moving water
5. Check regularly

### Bottle Trap

1. Cut plastic bottle in half
2. Invert top into bottom
3. Add bait
4. Secure together
5. Submerge in water

## Spearfishing

- Sharpen stick into point
- Wait quietly near vegetation
- Move slowly
- Strike quickly when fish appears

## Tips

- Fish are most active early morning and late afternoon
- Look for slow pools, eddies, undercut banks
- Bait works better than lures in survival situations
- Minnows and small fish are easier to catch

:::tip
Look for fish in slow-moving water, under overhanging branches, near rocks, and in deep pools.
:::
