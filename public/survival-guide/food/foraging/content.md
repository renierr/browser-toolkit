# Basic Foraging

Finding edible food in the wild requires knowledge and caution. Many plants are edible, but some are deadly poisonous.

## Golden Rules

1. **Never eat anything you cannot positively identify**
2. **When in doubt, don't eat it**
3. **Start with small amounts** to test for reactions
4. **Learn a few reliable plants thoroughly** rather than many uncertain ones

## Easy-to-Identify Edibles

### Dandelions (Taraxacum officinale)

- **Identification**: Yellow flowers, jagged leaves, white puffball seed head
- **Edible**: Leaves, flowers, roots
- **Preparation**: Leaves raw in salads or cooked; roots can be roasted

### Cattails (Typha)

- **Identification**: Brown cigar-shaped spikes, long grass-like leaves
- **Edible**: Roots (best), young shoots, pollen
- **Preparation**: Roots can be roasted or boiled; shoots cooked like asparagus

### Plantain (Plantago)

- **Identification**: Broad leaves with parallel veins, grows low to ground
- **Edible**: Leaves (use young ones)
- **Preparation**: Raw in salads or cooked

### Wild Onion/Garlic

- **Identification**: Grass-like leaves, smell of onion/garlic when crushed
- **Edible**: Bulbs, leaves
- **Preparation**: Raw or cooked

### Berries (General)

Safe berries include:

- **Blackberries/Raspberries**: Thorny canes, distinct berries
- **Blueberries**: Blue berries on shrub
- **Strawberries**: Small red berries, three-leaf pattern

## Danger: Don't Eat

- **White berries**: Almost always poisonous
- **Mushrooms**: Unless expert, don't eat any
- **Plants with milky sap**: Usually poisonous
- **Plants with umbrella-shaped flowers**: Many are poisonous

## Preparation Tips

- **Cook to reduce toxins**: Many plants are safer cooked
- **Boil**: Can remove bitter/poisonous compounds
- **Roast roots**: Makes many edible
- **Use multiple tests**: Try small amount, wait hours, try more

## Where to Find Food

- **Water sources**: Streams, rivers, lakes attract life and edible plants
- **Edges**: Forest edges, meadows
- **Sunny areas**: Most edible plants need sun
- **Disturbed ground**: Clearings, former campsites

## Quick Calorie Sources

- **Nuts and seeds**: If you can find them
- **Roots**: Cattails, burdock, dandelion
- **Insects**: High protein, but requires catching
- **Birds**: Hard to catch without equipment
