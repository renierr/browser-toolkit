# Fire Tinder

Tinder is the dry, finely divided material that catches a spark and begins to burn. Without good tinder, even the best fire-starting method will fail.

## Natural Tinder Options

- **Dry grass**: The finer, the better
- **Cattail fluff**: Excellent tinder, catches spark easily
- **Birch bark**: Inner bark works even when slightly damp
- **Dry moss**: Pull apart into fine strands
- **Plant down**: Milkweed, mullein, or dandelion seeds
- **Dead fern fronds**: Fine and dry

:::tip
The key word is DRY. Wet tinder will not catch a spark no matter how good your fire-starting method.
:::

## Preparing Tinder

1. **Gather** dry material from protected areas
2. **Pull apart** into fine, fluffy strands
3. **Create a bird's nest** shape with the tinder
4. **Store in a dry place** until needed

## Making Feather Sticks

For wet conditions, create feather sticks:

1. Find a dry softwood stick (cedar, pine)
2. With a knife, shave thin curls
3. Leave curls attached at one end
4. The curls catch sparks easily

## Improvised Tinder

- Cotton balls (from first aid kit)
- Dryer lint
- Paper (newspaper, toilet paper)
- Char cloth
- Petroleum jelly coated cotton balls

:::warning
Always carry dry tinder in a waterproof container. Your life may depend on it.
:::
