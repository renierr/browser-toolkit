# Wilderness Hygiene

In a survival situation, hygiene is often ignored, but poor sanitation leads to infections, sickness, and debilitating conditions like trench foot or chafing. 

## Hand Washing Without Soap

Keeping your hands clean prevents the ingestion of pathogens when handling food.
1. **Ash and Water**: Hardwood campfires produce white ash. Mixing white ash with water creates a crude, slippery alkaline substance (lye) that cuts through grease and kills bacteria.
2. **Sand and Mud**: Use fine sand or clay mud as an abrasive to scrub away dirt and oils, then rinse with the cleanest water available.

## Dental Care

Tooth infections can become life-threatening in the wild.
1. **Chewing Sticks**: Cut a small green twig from a non-toxic tree (like Willow, Birch, or Dogwood). Strip the bark from one end and chew it until the fibers splay out like a brush. Use it to scrub your teeth.
2. **Charcoal Toothpaste**: Finely crushed campfire charcoal acts as a natural abrasive and absorbs toxins. Rub it on your teeth and rinse.

## Body Maintenance

- **Crotch, Armpits, and Feet**: These areas accumulate sweat and bacteria, leading to extreme chafing or fungal infections. Wash these areas daily, even with just a damp cloth.
- **Trench Foot Prevention**: Always keep your feet dry. If your socks are wet, remove them, dry your feet by the fire, and let the socks dry. Sleeping with wet feet is a fast track to tissue damage.

## Field Expedient Toilet (Cat Hole)

Proper waste disposal prevents the spread of disease (like Giardia and Cholera) into your water supply.
1. **Location**: Go at least 200 feet (about 70 adult steps) away from any water source, camp, or trail.
2. **Dig a Cat Hole**: Dig a hole 6 to 8 inches deep (15-20 cm).
3. **Wiping Materials**: Use broad, non-toxic, non-hairy leaves (like maple or fern), smooth stones, or snow. Always test a leaf on a less sensitive area (like your arm) to ensure you aren't allergic.
4. **Bury It**: Fill the hole with the excavated dirt and pack it down.

:::warning
Never dispose of human waste uphill from a water source. Runoff will contaminate the area downstream.
:::
