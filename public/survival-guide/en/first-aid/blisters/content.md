# Treating Blisters

In a survival or bug-out scenario, your feet are your transportation. A simple blister can become infected or cause you to alter your gait, leading to severe joint pain and immobility.

## Prevention (The Best Cure)

- **Break in boots** before you need them.
- **Keep feet dry**: Change socks if they get wet or overly sweaty. 
- **Wear two pairs of socks**: A thin, synthetic liner sock with a thicker wool sock over it. The friction happens between the socks, not against your skin.
- **Address hot spots immediately**: The moment you feel irritation, STOP. Apply moleskin, medical tape, or duct tape to the area.

## Treating an Unbroken Blister

If the blister hasn't popped:

1. Clean the area gently with soap and water or alcohol wipes.
2. If it's small and not painful, **do not pop it**. The intact skin is a sterile environment.
3. Protect it with a donut-shaped piece of moleskin (cut a hole in the middle) to keep pressure off the blister bubble.
4. Cover the moleskin with a breathable bandage or tape.

## Treating a Large, Painful Blister

If the blister is large, painful, and likely to pop on its own during travel, it's better to drain it in a controlled manner:

1. Wash your hands and the blister area thoroughly.
2. Sterilize a needle with alcohol, boiling water, or holding it over a flame until it glows red (let it cool).
3. Puncture the blister at the very edge (bottom edge if possible, so gravity helps drain).
4. Gently press the fluid out with a clean gauze pad.
5. **DO NOT remove the loose skin** - it serves as a natural, sterile bandage.
6. Apply antibacterial ointment.
7. Cover with a sterile dressing or moleskin.

:::error
If you see red streaks traveling up your leg from the blister, or if it smells bad and oozes thick yellow/green pus, you have a serious infection requiring immediate medical attention.
:::

## Checklist
- [ ] Stop walking at the first sign of a "hot spot"
- [ ] Clean the feet and change into dry socks
- [ ] Only pop blisters if absolutely necessary for mobility
- [ ] Sterilize tools before puncturing
