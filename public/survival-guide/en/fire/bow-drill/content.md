# Bow Drill Fire Starting

The bow drill is one of the most effective primitive fire-starting methods. It uses friction to generate an ember from a wooden spindle and fireboard.

## What You Need

- **Spindle**: A straight, dry, softwood stick (willow, cedar, cottonwood)
- **Fireboard**: A flat piece of dry, softwood about 1 inch thick
- **Bow**: A curved stick with a cord attached
- **Socket/Handhold**: A hard stone, shell, or piece of hardwood
- **Cord**: Natural fiber string or vine
- **Tinder**: Dry grass, bark fibers, or cattail fluff

:::tip
Keep everything dry - moisture is your enemy when making fire.
:::

## Steps

1. **Prepare the fireboard**
   - Cut a small depression near the edge of the fireboard

2. **Create the spindle**
   - Carve the spindle to about 9 inches long
   - Make it roughly 3/8 inch diameter
   - Taper both ends slightly

3. **Make the notch**
   - Cut a V-shaped notch from the edge of the fireboard to the depression

4. **Position the spindle**
   - Place the spindle in the depression with the notch pointing toward you

5. **Attach the bow string**
   - Wrap the cord around the spindle once in a loop

6. **Apply the socket**
   - Press the socket firmly on top of the spindle

7. **Start bowing**
   - Use long, smooth strokes to spin the spindle rapidly

8. **Maintain pressure**
   - Apply downward pressure with the socket while bowing

9. **Create an ember**
   - After 20-30 seconds of steady bowing, smoke will appear
   - A small black ember will form in the depression

10. **Transfer to tinder**
    - Carefully move the glowing ember to your tinder bundle

11. **Nurture the flame**
    - Gently blow on the ember until it ignites

:::warning
Work in a clear, fire-safe area. Keep water nearby when practicing.
:::

## Practice Checklist

- [ ] Gather all materials before starting
- [ ] Ensure wood is dry
- [ ] Create proper notch in fireboard
- [ ] Maintain consistent bowing rhythm
- [ ] Transfer ember quickly to tinder

:::tip
The spindle should be dry softwood for best results. Use a rocking motion rather than just pushing.
:::
