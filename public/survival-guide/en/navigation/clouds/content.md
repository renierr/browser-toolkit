# Cloud Reading

Clouds can tell you about weather coming. Learn to read them for advance warning.

## Cloud Types and What They Mean

### Cumulus (puffy, white)

Fair weather. If they grow tall, expect thunderstorms.

### Cumulonimbus (tall, dark)

Thunderstorm clouds. Rain, lightning, strong winds coming.

### Cirrus (thin, wispy)

High clouds. May indicate approaching weather system within 24-48 hours.

### Stratus (low, gray layer)

Overcast skies, possible drizzle or fog.

### Nimbostratus (dark, low)

Continuous rain or snow.

## Signs of Developing Storms

- [ ] Clouds building vertically (towering)
- [ ] Darkening from below
- [ ] Anvil-shaped tops
- [ ] Greenish tint to clouds
- [ ] Clouds moving quickly

## Using Clouds for Direction

- Clouds generally move with weather systems
- In mid-latitudes, weather moves west to east
- Cloud movement can indicate wind direction at altitude

:::tip
Watch clouds over time. Their movement, growth, and change tell a story about coming weather.
:::
