# Snow Cave

A properly built snow cave can be 50-60°F warmer than outside. Snow is an excellent insulator when properly handled.

## Requirements

- **Snow depth**: At least 4 feet deep
- **Snow quality**: Must be able to pack - not powdery
- **Tools**: Stick, snowshoe, or anything to dig with

## Choosing the Site

1. **Deep snow**: At least 4 feet deep
2. **Protected area**: Away from wind and avalanche danger
3. **South-facing**: If possible, for maximum sun
4. **Near wood**: For fire and cooking

## Building Steps

### 1. Mark the Entrance

1. Decide on cave size (see dimensions below)
2. Mark a circle for entrance - about 3 feet diameter
3. Entrance should be below the sleeping platform level

### 2. Dig the Entrance

1. Start digging a tunnel downward
2. Angle down for at least 2 feet
3. This creates cold air trap below sleeping area

### 3. Hollow the Main Chamber

1. Dig out the main space
2. Keep walls at least 12 inches thick
3. Ceiling should be dome-shaped
4. Shape like a bowl - rounded top and bottom

### 4. Create Sleeping Platform

1. Raise platform 1-2 feet above floor
2. Cold air sinks below
3. Platform should be at body's height

### 5. Create Ventilation

1. Poke a small hole in the ceiling with a stick
2. About the size of your thumb
3. Critical to prevent CO2 buildup
4. Re-check and clear regularly

### 6. Finish the Floor

1. Pack snow smooth
2. Add pine boughs or debris for insulation
3. Cover with ground sheet if available

## Dimensions

- **One person**: 7 feet long x 5 feet wide x 5 feet tall
- **Two people**: 8 feet long x 6 feet wide x 5 feet tall
- Larger is harder to heat - build small

## Important Tips

- **Wet clothes become death traps** - Air out or change before entering
- **One match can heat it** - Be careful of melting ceiling
- **Dig entrance low** - Cold air stays below
- **Ventilate** - Always keep vent hole open
- **Not for extreme wind** - Blizzard can collapse entrance

## Warning Signs

- Cave starts to sag or drip - too warm, reduce fire
- Strong condensation - need more ventilation
- Difficulty breathing - open vent immediately

## Emergency Alternative: Snow Trench

If no time for cave:

1. Dig trench 2 feet deep, 6 feet long
2. Cover with branches and snow
3. Sleep in sleeping bag if available
4. Much less ideal but better than nothing
