# Boiling Water

Boiling is the most reliable method to make water safe to drink. It kills bacteria, viruses, and parasites.

## Steps

1. **Find a container**: Use any heat-safe container (metal pot, tin can, bottle in emergency)

2. **Collect water**: Fill your container with the clearest water available

3. **Build a fire**: Create a steady fire that can maintain heat

4. **Bring to boil**: Heat water until it reaches a rolling boil

5. **Maintain boil**: Keep water at a rolling boil for:
   - **1 minute** at elevations below 6,500 feet (2,000m)
   - **3 minutes** at higher elevations

6. **Cool naturally**: Let water cool naturally - do not add cold water

7. **Store properly**: Keep in clean container

## Important Notes

- Boiling does NOT remove chemical contaminants
- Clear water is easier to boil and uses less fuel
- Let water settle before boiling to reduce sediment
- A rolling boil (not just bubbling) is necessary

## Alternative Boiling Methods

### Hot Rocks

1. Heat rocks in your fire
2. Carefully place hot rocks into water container
3. Replace rocks as they cool
4. Continue until water boils

### Solar Boiling (Slow)

1. Fill a black container with water
2. Place in direct sunlight
3. Leave for several hours
4. Water will become pasteurized (not full boil but kills pathogens)

## Fuel Conservation

- Cover the pot to retain heat
- Use a lid with small hole for ventilation
- Keep fire hot, not roaring
- Boil what you need - excess water requires more fuel
