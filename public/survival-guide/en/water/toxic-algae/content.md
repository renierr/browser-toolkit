# Toxic Algae & Red Tide

While most survival water advice focuses on killing bacteria and filtering mud, there is one category of water hazard that standard survival techniques cannot defeat: **Toxic Algae Blooms (Cyanobacteria)**.

## The Danger

Toxic algae produce extremely powerful neurotoxins and hepatotoxins (liver poisons). 
- Boiling the water **does not destroy the toxins**. In fact, boiling bursts the algae cells, releasing *more* toxin into the water.
- Standard survival filters (like Lifestraws or DIY sand/charcoal filters) **do not filter out the chemical toxins**.
- Iodine and Chlorine drops **do not neutralize the toxins**.

If you drink water contaminated heavily with cyanobacteria, liver failure and death can occur rapidly. Likewise, foraging shellfish from coastal areas experiencing a "Red Tide" (marine toxic algae blooms) causes Paralytic Shellfish Poisoning.

## Identifying Toxic Freshwater Blooms (Cyanobacteria)

Algae blooms thrive in stagnant, warm lakes, ponds, and slow-moving rivers, especially during hot summers.

**Visual Signs of Poisonous Algae:**
1. **The "Spilled Paint" Look**: The water looks like someone poured neon green, blue-green, or bright blue paint on the surface.
2. **Pea Soup**: The water is extremely turbid with a thick, green, powdery suspension that looks like pea soup.
3. **Surface Scum**: Thick, mat-like scum on the surface that may have a foul, swampy, or garbage-like odor.
4. **Dead Wildlife**: Dead frogs, fish, or waterfowl floating near the shore.

## Identifying Safe Freshwater Plants

Do not confuse toxic single-celled cyanobacteria with safe, larger aquatic plants.
- **Duckweed**: Small, distinct green leaves floating independently on the surface. (Safe, harmless).
- **Filamentous Green Algae**: Stringy, hair-like mats that you can pick up with a stick. It looks like wet green hair. (Harmless).
- *Toxic algae*, by contrast, cannot be picked up with a stick; it will just slip through like colored water.

## Survival Protocol

1. **Avoid completely**: If you suspect a water source has a toxic algae bloom, do not drink it, do not touch it, and do not let your dog drink it.
2. **Do not eat the fish**: Toxins accumulate in the organs of fish living in the affected water.
3. **Seek moving water**: Toxins thrive in stagnant water. Walk upriver to find moving streams, or seek out ground springs and dig seep wells.
