# Improvised Water Filters

When you need clean water but have no filter, you can make effective filters from natural or available materials.

## Bottle Filter

### Materials

- Plastic bottle (cut in half)
- Sand (fine and coarse)
- Gravel or small rocks
- Charcoal (crushed)
- Cloth or grass

### Steps

1. Invert top half into bottom half
2. Add cloth at bottom (prevents sand from escaping)
3. Add crushed charcoal (2-3 inches)
4. Add fine sand (2 inches)
5. Add coarse sand (2 inches)
6. Add small gravel (1-2 inches)
7. Pour water through slowly

:::tip
Charcoal is crucial - it absorbs impurities and improves taste. Crush it small.
:::

## Emergency Filter (No Bottle)

### Using Natural Materials

1. Dig hole near water source
2. Line hole with grass, leaves
3. Add layers of sand and charcoal
4. Let water seep through
5. Collect filtered water from bottom

### Using Cloth

1. Multiple layers of cloth
2. Sandwich charcoal between layers
3. Pour water through
4. Repeat for cleaner water

## Pre-Filtering

Before filtering:

- [ ] Strain through cloth to remove large debris
- [ ] Let sediment settle
- [ ] Use clearest water available
- [ ] Filter multiple times

:::warning
Filter and then BOIL or treat. Filters remove particles but not all pathogens.
:::
