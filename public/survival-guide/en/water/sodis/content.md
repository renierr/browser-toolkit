# Solar Water Disinfection (SODIS)

SODIS uses sunlight to kill pathogens in water. Simple, effective, and requires no fuel.

## What You Need

- Clear plastic bottle (best) or glass jar
- Water
- Sunlight

## Steps

1. **Fill bottle** with clear water (leave 10% empty)
2. **Clean bottle** - remove labels, ensure transparency
3. **Check for damage** - scratches reduce effectiveness
4. **Place in sun** - direct sunlight for 6+ hours
5. **Flip occasionally** - ensures all water is exposed

## Timing

### Sunny Days

- 6 hours in direct sunlight

### Cloudy Days

- 2 consecutive days (48 hours total)

### High Altitude

- Increase time by 50% (more UV)

:::tip
The heat helps - hot water disinfects faster. Black containers speed the process.
:::

## Effectiveness

SODIS kills:

- Bacteria (E. coli, Cholera)
- Viruses (Rotavirus)
- Protozoa (Giardia)

Does NOT remove:

- Chemical contaminants
- Sediment
- Heavy metals

## Tips

- [ ] Use clear, undamaged plastic
- [ ] Lay flat for maximum sun exposure
- [ ] Flip halfway through
- [ ] Use dark bottle for faster results
- [ ] Pre-filter very cloudy water first
