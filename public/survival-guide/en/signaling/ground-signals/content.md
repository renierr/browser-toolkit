# Ground-to-Air Signals

Large signals visible from aircraft can save your life. Create these signals on open ground.

## Universal Distress Signals

Make signals at least 10 feet wide, 3 feet thick. Use materials that contrast with ground.

| Signal | Meaning                     |
| ------ | --------------------------- |
| V      | Require assistance          |
| X      | Require medical help        |
| I      | Require supplies            |
| →      | Traveling in this direction |

## How to Create Signals

1. **Choose location**
   - Open area visible from air
   - Near your shelter but not blocking escape

2. **Use available materials**
   - Rocks, logs, branches
   - Clothing, gear
   - Burned areas (charcoal)

3. **Make large**
   - At least 10 feet wide
   - Thick, visible lines

4. **Add movement**
   - Wave white cloth
   - Signal mirror
   - Fire for attention

:::tip
Aircraft will often circle back if they see you. Don't give up if they pass by.
:::
