# Signal Mirror

A signal mirror can be seen for miles, even in daylight. It's one of the most effective daytime signaling devices.

## What Can Be Used

- **Signal mirror** (best - designed for this purpose)
- **Car rearview mirror**
- **Phone screen** (reflects, but not ideal)
- **Metal object** (belt buckle, ID tag, credit card - limited effectiveness)
- **Camera lens** (back of camera, not front)

## How It Works

1. **Reflect sunlight** toward target
2. **Beam can travel miles**
3. **Works during day** (needs sunlight)
4. **Highly visible** against natural backgrounds

## Steps to Use

### 1. Find the Target

- Look for aircraft, boats, or people
- Aircraft are best - planes can see glints for miles
- Scan horizon with binoculars if available

### 2. Position Yourself

- Face the sun (but don't look at it)
- Target should be between you and sun, or to the side

### 3. Aim the Mirror

- Hold mirror close to eye
- Look past the reflection to your target
- "Paint" the target with the reflection

### 4. Create the Flash

- The reflection should hit your target
- A flash will be visible at great distances
- Move mirror in small circles or figure-8 to attract attention

### 5. Signal Protocol

- **Long flashes**: 3 seconds on, 3 seconds off
- **Continuous**: If aircraft is approaching
- **Follow aircraft**: If it passes, signal again if it turns away

## Tips for Effectiveness

- **Practice** before you need it
- **Keep polished**: Clean mirror works better
- **Hand signals**: Wave bright object if mirror unavailable
- **Shiny objects work**: Even a white piece of paper can create flash
- **At night**: Shine at pilots (headlights direct attention)

## Limitations

- Needs direct sunlight
- Requires line of sight
- Effective range decreases in fog, haze
- Clouds diffuse light but don't block it completely

## Alternative: Flashlight at Night

- Point at aircraft
- Flash in sequence (3 short, 3 long)
- Move in circles to be more visible
- Works well with focused beam

## Safety

- Never look directly at sun in mirror
- Don't aim at aircraft cockpit - flash can temporarily blind
- Be aware of your surroundings when signaling
