# Fire Signals

Fire is one of the most effective ways to signal for rescue. Use signals that will be noticed from a distance.

## The Three-Fire Signal

The universal distress signal is **three fires** arranged in a triangle or line:

1. **Build three fires**
2. **Space them about 100 feet apart** (30 meters)
3. **Make them in clear area** visible from above
4. **Keep them burning** until aircraft or rescue is spotted

## Signal Fire Tips

### Location

- Choose high ground if possible
- Clear area away from trees
- Near rescue route if known

### Materials

- **Green vegetation**: Creates thick smoke (daytime)
- **Dry wood**: Creates bright flame (nighttime)
- **Rubber or plastic**: Creates black smoke (if available)

### Making Smoke

1. Build large fire
2. Add green vegetation when fire is hot
3. Cover with damp leaves or grass
4. This creates thick smoke pillar

### At Night

- Make fire as large as possible
- Use dry wood for maximum brightness
- Create contrast - clear dark ground around fire
- Build near water for reflection

## Ground-to-Air Signals

Use rocks, logs, or cleared ground to create signals:

| Signal       | Meaning                           |
| ------------ | --------------------------------- |
| V            | Require assistance                |
| X            | Require medical help              |
| I            | Require supplies                  |
| Arrow (→)    | Traveling in this direction       |
| Arrow + line | Going this direction to find help |

### Creating Signals

- Make signals at least 10 feet wide
- Use materials that contrast with ground
- Place in open areas visible from air
- Near your location but not blocking escape

## Smoke Signals

1. Cover fire with green vegetation
2. Let smoke billow up
3. Release in three bursts for distress

## Tips for Visibility

- **Fire**: Can be seen for miles at night
- **Smoke**: Visible for miles during day
- **Light reflection**: Can be seen for 10+ miles with mirror
- **Movement**: Waving arms or cloth helps attract attention
- **Sound**: Whistle, yell - three is the magic number

## Timing

- **Aircraft**: Watch for planes, signal when you hear them
- **Regular intervals**: Don't exhaust yourself
- **Maintain**: Keep signals ready to light
- **At night**: Build fire, wait for aircraft, light when heard
