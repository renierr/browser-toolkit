# Cooking Without a Pot

Learn to cook food even without traditional cookware. Improvisation is key in survival situations.

## Improvised Cookware

- **Canted rocks**: Heat rocks, place food on them
- **Hot rock cooking**: Wrap food in leaves, place on hot rocks
- **Coals**: Bury food in hot coals (like baked potatoes)
- **Stick roasting**: Cook directly over fire on stick

## Boiling Water Without a Pot

### Rock Boiling

1. Heat rocks in fire until very hot
2. Carefully transfer to water container
3. Use long sticks or pliers
4. Replace cooled rocks with hot ones
5. Repeat until water boils

### Log Boiling (for small amounts)

1. Find a dry, thick-walled log
2. Carve out center to create bowl shape
3. Fill with water
4. Heat with hot rocks from inside and below
5. Works for small amounts

## Cooking Food

### Pit Cooking

1. Dig hole, build fire
2. Place hot coals, then food wrapped in leaves
3. Cover with more leaves, then dirt
4. Cooks slowly over several hours

### Spit Roasting

1. Skewer food on green stick
2. Hold over fire or prop at edge
3. Rotate regularly for even cooking
4. Works for meat, fish, vegetables

### Stone Frying

1. Heat flat rock in fire
2. Use as frying surface
3. Add fat if available to prevent sticking

:::tip
Always cook meat thoroughly. Burning on outside is better than raw inside.
:::
