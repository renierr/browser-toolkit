# Cleaning and Filleting Fish

Once you have successfully caught a fish, properly cleaning it is essential to prevent the meat from spoiling and to avoid puncturing the internal organs, which can ruin the flesh.

## Tools Needed
- A sharp knife (fillet knife is ideal, but any sharp blade works)
- Clean water
- A flat rock or log to use as a cutting board

## Step 1: Humane Dispatch
Before starting, ensure the fish is dead. A sharp strike to the top of the head (just above the eyes) with a solid stick or rock is the most humane method.

## Step 2: Scaling (If cooking whole)
If you plan to cook the fish whole (with skin on), you need to remove the scales.
1. Hold the fish by the tail.
2. Using the back (dull side) of your knife, scrape firmly from the tail toward the gills.
3. Rinse the fish periodically.

## Step 3: Gutting
1. Insert the tip of your knife into the vent (the small hole near the base of the tail on the belly).
2. Cut upward along the belly all the way to the gills. **Do not cut too deep, or you will puncture the intestines.**
3. Reach in and pull all the internal organs out. They should come out in one connected mass.
4. Using your thumb, scrape out the dark red bloodline (kidney) that runs along the spine inside the cavity.
5. Rinse the cavity thoroughly with clean water.

## Step 4: Removing the Head and Fins (Optional)
Make a firm cut right behind the gills and pectoral fins straight down through the spine. You can slice off the dorsal and bottom fins as well.

:::tip
Don't throw away the head, bones, and guts immediately if trapping! Fish guts make excellent bait for traps, and the bones/head can be boiled to make a nutrient-rich fish broth.
:::

## Step 5: Filleting (Alternative Method)
If you want boneless meat without gutting:
1. Make a deep cut behind the gills down to the backbone.
2. Turn the blade flat and slice along the backbone all the way to the tail.
3. Flip the fillet skin-side down. Hold the tail edge and run your knife between the skin and meat to separate them.
4. Flip the fish over and repeat for the other side.

## Hygiene Checklist
- [ ] Wash hands and knife frequently
- [ ] Keep the meat cool and out of direct sunlight
- [ ] Dispose of unused guts away from your camp to avoid attracting predators
