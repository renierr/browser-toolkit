# Following Water

Water is one of the most reliable navigation tools in the wilderness. Following water will often lead to civilization.

## Why Water Works

- **Gravity**: Water flows downhill
- **Civilization**: People settle near water
- **Roads**: Roads often follow rivers
- **Agriculture**: Farmland needs water

## Types of Water to Follow

### Rivers and Streams

1. **Follow downstream**: Leads to larger bodies, more people
2. **Follow upstream**: Leads to smaller streams, sometimes to springs
3. **Major rivers**: Almost always lead to towns/cities
4. **Confluence**: Where streams join - often good spots for camps

###creeks and Brooks

- Smaller than rivers but still lead somewhere
- Good to follow if no major streams available

## Using Water Navigation

### Finding Streams from High Ground

1. Climb to high ground if possible
2. Look for valleys and low areas
3. Follow valleys down
4. Listen for running water

### Following a Stream

1. Follow downstream for civilization
2. Look for signs: roads, bridges, cleared land
3. Listen for sounds of human activity
4. Watch for fishing lines or other human items

### When to Follow Water

- When you need water for drinking
- When trying to reach civilization
- When other navigation methods fail
- When in unknown terrain

## Potential Dangers

- **River crossings**: Can be dangerous or impossible
- **Canyons**: Can trap you - don't go deep into canyons
- **Swamps**: Can be impassable
- **Distance**: Can take longer than overland routes

## Signs of Civilization

- **Muddy or disturbed banks**: Animals and humans
- **Cut vegetation**: Beavers, humans clearing
- **Fishing equipment**: Lines, nets
- **Roads or trails**: Leading to or from water
- **Bridges**: Clear sign of human presence
- **Agricultural land**: Fields, fences

## Combining Methods

- Follow water but stay aware of direction
- Use sun/stars to maintain general heading
- Use high ground to see surroundings
- If water leads to obstacle, climb out in direction you want to go

## Alternatives

If water isn't available:

- Follow valleys downhill
- Look for animal trails (often lead to water)
- Listen for sounds of civilization
- Head toward lower elevations in general
