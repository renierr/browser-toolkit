# Making an Improvised Compass

A compass helps you find direction. Here's how to make a basic compass with minimal materials.

## Method 1: Magnetized Needle

### What You Need

- Needle or piece of steel
- Magnet (or silk, wool, or Earth's field)
- Something to float on (leaf, cork, paper)

### Steps

1. **Magnetize the needle**
   - Rub one direction on magnet 50+ times
   - Or rub on silk/wool vigorously

2. **Create floater**
   - Place needle on leaf, cork, or paper

3. **Place in water**
   - The needle will align north-south
   - Wait for it to settle

## Method 2: Shadow Compass

1. Place stick in ground
2. Mark tip of shadow
3. Wait 15-20 minutes
4. Mark new position
5. Line between marks is roughly east-west

## Method 3: Watch Compass

1. Point hour hand at sun
2. Point between hour hand and 12 is south (Northern Hemisphere)

:::tip
Practice these methods before you need them. In survival, you want skills, not experiments.
:::
