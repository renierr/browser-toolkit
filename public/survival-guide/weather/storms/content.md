# Storm Warning Signs

Knowing how to read weather signs can help you prepare for storms before they arrive.

## Cloud Signs of Approaching Storms

### High Clouds (6+ miles up)

- **Cirrus**: Thin, wispy clouds - may indicate approaching front within 24-48 hours
- **Cirrostratus**: Thin, sheet-like clouds - rain within 12-24 hours

### Middle Clouds (2-6 miles up)

- **Altostratus**: Gray, sheet clouds - continuous rain likely within hours

### Low Clouds (below 2 miles)

- **Nimbostratus**: Dark, rain-bearing clouds - precipitation likely soon
- **Stratus**: Gray, featureless layer - drizzle or light rain

## Storm Development Signs

- [ ] Clouds building vertically (cumulonimbus)
- [ ] Darkening sky, greenish tint
- [ ] Rapidly falling barometer
- [ ] Increasing wind
- [ ] Distant rumbling thunder
- [ ] Sudden drop in temperature

## Lightning Safety

1. **If you feel hair standing up**: Crouch immediately
2. **Avoid high points**: Don't be the highest object
3. **Avoid solitary trees**: Find low area
4. **Stay away from metal**: Tents, fences, equipment
5. **If in vehicle**: Stay inside, close windows

:::warning
The 30-30 rule: If lightning-to-thunder time is under 30 seconds, seek shelter. Stay inside for 30 minutes after last thunder.
:::

## Before the Storm

- [ ] Secure loose items
- [ ] Check supplies
- [ ] Fill containers with water
- [ ] Charge devices
- [ ] Know your shelter location
