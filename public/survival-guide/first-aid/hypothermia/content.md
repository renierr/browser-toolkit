# Hypothermia

Hypothermia occurs when body temperature drops below 95°F (35°C). It's caused by prolonged exposure to cold and can be life-threatening.

## Signs of Hypothermia

### Mild (90-95°F / 32-35°C)

- Shivering (body trying to generate heat)
- Confusion
- Slurred speech
- Loss of coordination
- Cold, pale skin

### Severe (below 90°F / 32°C)

- Shivering stops (body has exhausted energy)
- Drowsiness
- Weakness
- Loss of consciousness
- Slow, shallow breathing

:::error
Severe hypothermia requires immediate emergency care. The person may appear dead but can sometimes be revived.
:::

## Immediate Actions

1. **Move to shelter** - Get out of wind and rain
2. **Remove wet clothing** - Wet clothes accelerate heat loss
3. **Add dry layers** - Cover head, neck, torso
4. **Provide heat** - Body heat, fire, warm water bottles
5. **Give warm drinks** - If conscious, not vomiting

## Treatment Steps

### 1. Stop Heat Loss

- Find or create shelter
- Block wind
- Remove wet clothing immediately

### 2. Warm the Person

- Skin-to-skin contact under blankets (last resort)
- Use fire or heat sources
- Warm center of body first (chest, neck, head)

### 3. Provide Energy

- Warm sweet drinks if conscious
- Food if possible
- Simple sugars help generate heat

:::warning
Do NOT rub or massage limbs - this can push cold blood to heart. Do NOT give alcohol - it increases heat loss.
:::

## Prevention

- [ ] Wear appropriate layers
- [ ] Stay dry
- [ ] Eat enough food
- [ ] Keep moving
- [ ] Check on companions regularly
