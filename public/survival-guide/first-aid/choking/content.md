# Choking

Choking occurs when an object blocks the airway. Quick action can save a life.

## Signs of Choking

- Hands clutched to throat (universal sign)
- Inability to speak or make sounds
- Weak or ineffective coughing
- Skin, lips, or nails turning blue
- Loss of consciousness

:::error
Act immediately. A person can die in minutes if completely blocked.
:::

## For Adults (Heimlich Maneuver)

### 1. Confirm Choking

- Ask: "Are you choking?"
- If they can't speak, nod yes

### 2. Position Behind Person

- Stand behind them
- Wrap arms around their waist

### 3. Make a Fist

- Place thumb side of fist above navel
- Below ribcage, above waist

### 4. Give Abdominal Thrusts

- Grasp fist with other hand
- Pull sharply upward and inward
- Repeat until object is expelled

### 5. If Person Becomes Unconscious

- Lower to ground
- Call for help
- Begin CPR
- Check mouth for visible object before breaths

## Self-Choking

If alone and choking:

1. Make a fist above navel
2. Grasp with other hand
3. Bend over a hard surface (chair back, railing)
4. Shove fist inward and upward

## For Children (1-8 years)

- Use same technique but with less force
- Position child accordingly
- Can also hold child upside down and give back blows

## For Infants (under 1 year)

1. Hold infant face down on forearm
2. Support head and neck
3. Give 5 back blows between shoulder blades
4. Turn infant over, give 5 chest thrusts
5. Repeat until object expelled

:::warning
Never do blind finger sweeps - you may push object further in.
:::
