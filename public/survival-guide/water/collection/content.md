# Water Collection

Finding and collecting water is critical for survival. Here are various methods to source water in the wilderness.

## Rainwater Collection

1. **Set out containers**: Place all available containers to catch rain
2. **Use large surfaces**: Tarps, ponchos, or sheets can channel water into containers
3. **Create funnel**: Shape plastic or bark into a funnel for small containers
4. **Dig channels**: Create small channels to direct water flow

## Dew Collection

1. **Early morning**: Collect dew before the sun rises
2. **Use cloth**: Drag a cloth through grass and wring into a container
3. ** absorbent materials**: Tie cloths around your ankles and walk through grass
4. **Vegetation**: Wipe moisture from plants

## River and Stream Water

1. **Look for moving water**: Moving water is generally cleaner
2. **Upstream**: Collect upstream from where you plan to camp
3. **Settle before drinking**: Let sediment settle before consuming
4. **Look for junctions**: Small streams often join larger ones

## Natural Springs

1. **Valley floors**: Look for springs in low-lying areas
2. **Rocky areas**: Water often seeps from rocky outcrops
3. **Vegetation**: Lush green vegetation can indicate water nearby
4. **Animal trails**: Animals know where water is - follow their paths

## Solar Still

1. **Dig a hole**: About 3 feet across, 2 feet deep
2. **Place container**: Put a container in the center
3. **Add vegetation**: Place green vegetation around the container
4. **Cover with plastic**: Place plastic sheet over the hole
5. **Seal edges**: Weight down with rocks or dirt
6. **Create low point**: Place a small rock in center so water drips into container

## Snow and Ice

- **Melt before drinking**: Never eat snow or ice directly
- **Use body heat**: Hold snow in your hands to melt
- **Cook over fire**: Melt snow in a container over heat
- **Rule of thumb**: 1 inch of snow equals about 1 inch of water

## Finding Water

- Follow terrain downhill
- Look for bird activity (especially at dawn and dusk)
- Watch for insects (bees never stray far from water)
- Look for lush vegetation
- Listen for running water
