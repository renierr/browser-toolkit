# Tropical Shelter

In tropical environments, protection from rain and insects is more important than warmth. Here's how to build a shelter in hot, humid conditions.

## Key Considerations

- **Rain**: Tropical rain can be intense and frequent
- **Insects**: Mosquitoes and other bugs are major concerns
- **Heat**: Need ventilation, not insulation
- **Materials**: Palm fronds, leaves, and vines are abundant

## The Poncho Lean-To

1. Find two supports (trees, poles)
2. Stretch poncho or tarp between them
3. Angle to shed rain
4. Create walls using leaves or additional material

## The Tropical Leaf Hut

1. Find or make a frame (lean-to or simple rectangle)
2. Layer large leaves (palm, banana) overlapping like shingles
3. Start from bottom, work up
4. Make roof thick enough to stop rain

:::tip
In tropical areas, focus on keeping rain off and allowing air flow. You don't need to worry about cold.
:::

## Making a Mosquito Bed

1. Find four sticks for corners
2. Lash together or jam into ground
3. Stretch cloth, poncho, or netting over frame
4. Sleep elevated from ground
5. Tuck edges in to prevent insects getting in
