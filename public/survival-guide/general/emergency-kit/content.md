# Emergency Kit Checklist

Being prepared can save your life. Here's what to include in a basic emergency/survival kit.

## Essentials

- [ ] Water (1 gallon per person per day)
- [ ] Non-perishable food
- [ ] Multi-tool or knife
- [ ] Fire starter (matches, lighter, ferro rod)
- [ ] Flashlight + extra batteries
- [ ] First aid kit
- [ ] Whistle
- [ ] Dust mask
- [ ] Cell phone + portable charger
- [ ] Important documents (copies)

## Shelter

- [ ] Tarp or emergency blanket
- [ ] Paracord (50 feet)
- [ ] Duct tape
- [ ] Sleeping bag or thermal blanket

## Navigation

- [ ] Compass
- [ ] Maps of area
- [ ] GPS device (if available)

## Tools

- [ ] Screwdriver
- [ ] Pliers
- [ ] Wrench
- [ ] Can opener
- [ ] Scissors

## Hygiene

- [ ] Sanitation supplies
- [ ] Moist towelettes
- [ ] Plastic bags

## Vehicle Kit (if applicable)

- [ ] Jumper cables
- [ ] Flashlight
- [ ] Tire repair kit
- [ ] Flares
- [ ] Ice scraper
- [ ] Blanket

:::tip
Check your kit twice a year. Replace expired items and update for seasonal needs.
:::

## Bug Out Bag (Quick Grab)

Keep a separate, lighter bag ready:

- [ ] Water (72 hours supply)
- [ ] Food (72 hours supply)
- [ ] First aid
- [ ] Multi-tool
- [ ] Flashlight
- [ ] Whistle
- [ ] Dust mask
- [ ] Tarp
- [ ] Radio
- [ ] Cash
