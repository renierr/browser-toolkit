# Whistle Signals

A whistle can be heard much farther than the human voice. It requires less energy than yelling and works in many situations.

## Why Use a Whistle

- Can be heard up to 1 mile away
- Requires less energy than yelling
- Works in conditions where voice doesn't carry
- Three short blasts is universal distress signal

## Standard Signals

### Distress Signal

- **Three short blasts**: "Help me!"
- **Three long blasts**: "Come here"
- **Repeat** at regular intervals

### Communication

- **One short**: "Yes" / "I'm here"
- **Two short**: "No"
- **One long**: Look at me / Pay attention

## Using Your Whistle

1. **Three is the magic number** - Use three of anything (blasts, fires, sounds)
2. **Wait for response** - After three blasts, wait 10-15 seconds
3. **Repeat** - Continue until you get a response

:::tip
Always carry a whistle - they're cheap, lightweight, and could save your life.
:::

## Improvised Whistle

If you don't have a whistle:

- Use your hands cupped around mouth
- Use a piece of hollow grass or reed
- Use a bottle
- Bang rocks together rhythmically

## Voice Signals

If no whistle available:

- **One long yell**: Help!
- **Wait 10 seconds**
- **Repeat** three times
- **Wait and listen** for response
